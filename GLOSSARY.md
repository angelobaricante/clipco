# Clipcon

Creators prepare knowledge about their raw footage so an editing agent can find and understand the material needed for a video.

## Language

**Creator**:
The person who records footage and specifies the intended video.

**Project**:
A collection of footage associated with a content idea or intended video.

**Source clip**:
An original recorded video file supplied by the creator.
_Avoid_: Edited video, segment

**Segment**:
A time-bounded portion of a source clip.
_Avoid_: Source clip

**A-roll**:
Footage carrying the main spoken explanation or narrative, commonly a talking-head recording.

**B-roll**:
Supporting footage that illustrates or complements the main narrative.

**Footage role**:
The narrative function of a Segment, such as A-roll or B-roll. A Segment can also contain mixed roles or need creator review; different Segments of one Source clip can have different roles.

**Footage library**:
The creator's collection of indexed footage, independent of any one Project. Its Source clips can belong to multiple Projects or remain in the library without a Project.

**Project membership**:
The association between a Source clip and a Project, including the creator's context and retrieval choices for that Project.

**Reusable B-roll**:
B-roll available for consideration in another content idea when it fits that video's narrative or intended emotional tone.

**Reuse permission**:
The creator's choice of whether a Source clip may be considered as B-roll outside its Project associations.

**Project exclusion**:
The creator's reversible choice to omit a Source clip's membership and Project-specific context from editing-agent retrieval for that Project.

**Emotional tone**:
A suggested emotional effect footage may have on a viewer in a particular narrative context. It is an interpretation, can have more than one plausible reading, and differs from the emotion visibly expressed by a depicted person.
_Avoid_: Guaranteed viewer reaction, emotion detection

**Analysis job**:
Queued work to prepare or enrich a Source clip's footage context, with progress and an outcome the creator can review.

**Footage context**:
Descriptions, speech, source references, and relevant relationships that help someone understand recorded material. Descriptions are interpretations of the footage, rather than a guarantee that every detail has been captured.
_Avoid_: Full understanding

**Repeated take**:
Another recorded attempt to express substantially the same point. Repetition alone does not determine which attempt the creator prefers.

**Spoken correction**:
A statement in the footage that revises an earlier statement. The earlier statement and its correction provide context for choosing the intended explanation.

**Footage index**:
A persistent, searchable collection of footage context for the Footage library and its Projects.

**Editing agent**:
An assistant such as Claude or Codex that uses footage context to make editing decisions and operate editing tools.
_Avoid_: Local footage assistant

**Local footage assistant**:
The product that understands and organizes footage on the creator's device and supplies context to the editing agent.
_Avoid_: Video editor
