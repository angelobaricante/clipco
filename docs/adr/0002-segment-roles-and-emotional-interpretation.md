# Assign footage roles to Segments and keep emotional tone interpretive

The creator accepted Segment-level footage roles and reuse eligibility, with creator correction and a clip-level browsing summary, because a single recording can contain both narrative and usable cutaways. Emotional tone remains an attributed, evidence-based interpretation with multiple possible readings, separate from observations and depicted-person emotion. A single clip role or definitive emotion label would hide useful footage and give subjective suggestions more authority than the evidence supports.

Mixed and Needs review ranges remain available for Project review but require clarification before reuse. Missing emotional analysis is explicitly Not analyzed, never a neutral-tone assumption.
