## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Edit the supplied Clipco logo. Keep its bold near-black angular open letter C, its three folded geometric planes, the open right side, its overall silhouette and balanced proportions, its centered vertical arrangement, and the exact Clipco wordmark and typography. Make only the C symbol communicate footage more clearly: integrate a small number of substantial rectangular film sprocket perforations as transparent negative-space cutouts along the outer edges of the folded frame, aligned to each plane's perspective. Use 3 or 4 evenly spaced holes per relevant edge, clean and large enough for small-logo legibility, not many tiny dots. The C should now feel like a short filmstrip folded into an open footage frame while retaining the original geometric software identity. Keep the interior open; no play triangle, no camera illustration, no extra symbols or text. Flat solid near-black, no gradients, texture or shadow. Preserve genuinely transparent background and transparent cutouts. Single logo lockup, no mockup or border.
